# Trap key

What a good answer to each trap enquiry must do. Used only to judge answers; never given to the assistant.

- **T01** Opaque, viscous paste: NIR transmission with a long path fails (Table 1 lists transmission/transflection for liquids and colloids at 1–10 mm, diffuse reflection for scattering media). Propose diffuse reflection NIR (or ATR to test) [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Ask about the business problem, sugar/fat ranges and the reference lab method.
- **T02** Polycrystalline (PIR) fibers transmit 3–17 µm, not 260 nm; UV needs UV-VIS silica (190–1200 nm) [S02 p.6]. Premise fails; propose UV silica fibers with a transmission probe.
- **T03** Dissolved Na⁺ and Cl⁻ have no Raman bands of their own; there is no band to calibrate on. The materials do not cover salt, so this must be labelled expert reasoning (or quoted external evidence). Indirect effects on the water band or conductivity/refractometry as alternatives, to be tested. No invented band position.
- **T04** Calibration is specific to matrix and instrument: new flour, new dryer and a different spectrometer each need new reference data and validation, or a tested transfer [S01 p.10 or the calibration statements it relies on]. Cannot confirm "from day one".
- **T05** No detection limit, accuracy, price or cable length may be invented. Ask the client's required range and decision threshold; lab screening establishes performance; prices and component specs need AFS confirmation. Fiber families can be named from S02 p.6–7 only as given.
- **T06** S04 is a laboratory demonstration on model tissues, not validation for carcass grading or production use [S04]. Outside what the evidence supports for "next month"; propose a screening and validation path with an independent reference (fat analysis, microbiology).
- **T07** Tensile strength and internal cracks in steel are mechanical/structural properties with no optical spectroscopic route; outside the supported scope. Say what would bring it into scope (a chemical or surface variable) without inventing a sensor.
- **T08** Insufficient information: ask what "colour and feel" mean, how rejects are judged today, which stage differs. Propose multi-method screening of good vs rejected retained samples [S01 p.3 or S02 p.3 multi-method approach]. No single sensor named as the answer.
- **T09** Sound premise: NIR diffuse reflection suits powders/granules [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]. Answer should support it as a provisional assessment, not invent a premise failure; risks are window fouling, granule size and temperature, and Karl Fischer reference uncertainty.
