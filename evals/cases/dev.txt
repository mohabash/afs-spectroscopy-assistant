Development enquiries D01-D08, copied verbatim from AFS_Examples_and_Reference.docx section 1.

==============================================================================
D01 | Blend potency in a tablet press feed frame
==============================================================================

We manufacture tablets from a powder blend containing an active pharmaceutical
ingredient and excipients. Laboratory checks tell us whether the blend is on
target, but results arrive after the material has moved through the press. We
want to assess whether a fiber based measurement in the feed frame could
follow API concentration changes. Our first pilot should compare reasonable
measurement options before we commit to production equipment.

==============================================================================
D02 | Fat and protein monitoring in opaque dairy product
==============================================================================

We produce an opaque, concentrated milk product that flows through a
processing line. We currently sample it for fat and protein analysis and would
like earlier information about composition changes. Product temperature and
texture vary during production. We want an initial spectroscopy concept and a
practical comparison experiment, while retaining our laboratory checks.

==============================================================================
D03 | A clear whey stream before concentration
==============================================================================

Our dairy process includes a relatively clear whey stream before a
concentration stage. We want to investigate whether spectroscopy can track
protein concentration as the stream changes, with laboratory results used as
the reference. The stream can become more turbid under some operating
conditions. We need a first measurement concept that handles this change
rather than assuming the liquid always remains clear.

==============================================================================
D04 | Glucose and lactate trends in an aqueous bioprocess
==============================================================================

We run a cell culture process in an aqueous medium. Glucose and lactate are
measured from samples, and the delay makes it difficult to see changes
promptly. We are exploring an in situ spectroscopy pilot that can be checked
against those existing laboratory measurements. The medium and cell population
change during the run; we have not yet selected a probe or a calibration
strategy.

==============================================================================
D05 | Biomass related monitoring during yeast fermentation
==============================================================================

We would like an optical measurement during yeast fermentation to help us
follow growth and notice changes earlier. Our team has mentioned both a
biomass signal and a metabolic signal, but these have not been defined as
separate targets. The broth becomes more optically dense as the run develops.
We need help turning this request into an initial feasibility study with
useful reference measurements.

==============================================================================
D06 | Water content in a petroleum stream
==============================================================================

We handle a petroleum stream and currently send samples to a laboratory for
water determination. We want to investigate a fiber based measurement closer
to the process so that changes can be noticed sooner. The analyzer would be
outside the process area. Before selecting equipment, we need to know what to
clarify and what a first comparison experiment should establish.

==============================================================================
D07 | API concentration during hot polymer extrusion
==============================================================================

We are developing a hot polymer extrusion process that incorporates an active
pharmaceutical ingredient. We want to investigate whether an optical
measurement can detect composition changes while the melt is being processed.
We have offline assays, but the process interface is hot and may be exposed to
material build up. We need an initial comparison plan and the questions
required before choosing a probe.

==============================================================================
D08 | Local measurements of heterogeneous model tissues
==============================================================================

Our research group is evaluating a spectral measurement interface using model
biological tissues such as chicken muscle, fat and liver. We want to compare
MIR ATR and fluorescence information, because the material is heterogeneous
and sequential measurements may not sample the same location. This is a
laboratory classification study. We need a first comparison experiment and a
clear statement of what its results would demonstrate.
