[Figure: Figure 3] Three scatter plots labeled (a), (b), and (c) showing PCA score plots for three chicken tissue types: fat (red points and light red shaded convex hull), breast (blue points and light blue shaded convex hull), and liver (brown points and light brown shaded convex hull), where different marker shapes (circles, squares, triangles) represent three different samples of each tissue type. In plot (a), the horizontal axis is labeled "PC1 (85.91%)" with tick marks at -2, 0, and 2, and the vertical axis is labeled "PC2 (11.13%)" with tick marks at -0.5, 0, 0.5, and 1; the red cluster is separated in the upper region while the blue and brown clusters overlap in the lower region. In plot (b), the horizontal axis is labeled "PC1 (59.25%)" with tick marks at -2, 0, and 2, and the vertical axis is labeled "PC2 (30.28%)" with tick marks at -2.5, 0, and 2.5; the red and blue clusters overlap on the left (around PC1 from -2 to 0) while the brown cluster is separated on the right (around PC1 = 2). In plot (c), the horizontal axis is labeled "PC1 (60.82%)" with tick marks at -3, -1, and 1, and the vertical axis is labeled "PC2 (19.54%)" with tick marks at -1.5, 0, and 1.5; all three tissue clusters (red, blue, and brown) are completely separated from one another.
