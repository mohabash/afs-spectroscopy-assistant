[Figure: Dysprosium Oxide Reflectance Spectralon] A line chart comparing two reflectance spectra plotted with "Wavenumbers cm-1" on the horizontal axis (ranging from 5000 to 10000 in increments of 500) and "Reflectance %" on the vertical axis (ranging from 0 to 100 in increments of 20). The legend shows two curves: "FT-NIR Spectrometer" (blue line, showing higher spectral resolution with sharper features) and "Diffraction Grating NIR Spectrometer" (orange/red line, showing a smoother profile). Both curves start slightly above 100% at 5000 cm-1, show absorption dips between 5500 and 6300 cm-1 (the blue curve dipping to about 86% near 5700 cm-1 and 72% near 5950 cm-1, while the orange curve dips to about 83% near 6000 cm-1), rise back to around 100% between 6500 and 7000 cm-1, drop into a deep absorption band reaching a minimum of about 38% near 7950 cm-1, and then rise to a broad plateau of 80%–92% between 8500 and 9500 cm-1 before approaching 96%–99% at 10000 cm-1.
