[Figure: Figure 8. Sensor data: (a) Series A, (b) Series B, and (c,d) the data mean and standard deviation in Series A and B, respectively. Tumor and normal tissue measurements are shown with red and green lines, respectively. (Reproduced with permission from authors [29]).] Four line charts labeled (a), (b), (c), and (d) arranged in a 2x2 grid. All four charts share the same axis labels and legend: the horizontal axis is labeled "sensor channels" with four tick marks at "0.94 µm (U4)", "1.17 µm (U3)", "1.30 µm (U1)", and "1.44 µm (U2)"; the vertical axis is labeled "V" with tick marks at "0", "0.5", "1", and "1.5"; and the legend in the top right corner of each plot shows a red line for "T" and a green line for "N". Subplots (a) and (b) display multiple individual measurement lines for Series A and Series B respectively, where red lines (T) span higher voltage values (reaching up to about 1.3 V in (a) and 1.5 V in (b) at 1.30 µm (U1)) and green lines (N) are clustered lower (mostly below 0.5 V). Subplots (c) and (d) show the mean lines surrounded by shaded standard deviation bands for Series A and Series B respectively: in (c), the red mean line (T) is at approximately 0.5 V at U4, 0.38 V at U3, 0.75 V at U1, and 0.13 V at U2, while the green mean line (N) is at approximately 0.21 V at U4, 0.17 V at U3, 0.31 V at U1, and 0.06 V at U2; in (d), the red mean line (T) is at approximately 0.45 V at U4, 0.30 V at U3, 0.52 V at U1, and 0.09 V at U2, while the green mean line (N) is at approximately 0.22 V at U4, 0.14 V at U3, 0.21 V at U1, and 0.05 V at U2.
