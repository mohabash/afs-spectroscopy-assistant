Analysis of near-infrared (NIR) spectra in Series A: (a) raw spectra, (b) second-derivative spectra, and (c) partial least-squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA) regression coefficients. In plot (b), the y-axis is labeled "Arbitary" [sic] with ticks at -0.03, -0.025, -0.02, -0.015, -0.01, -0.005, 0, 0.005, 0.01, and 0.015, and includes the same "T" (red) and "N" (green) legend in the top-right corner; the curves oscillate around a horizontal baseline at 0, with prominent positive peaks in the red curves around 930 nm (up to ~0.01), 1150 nm (up to ~0.009), 1390 nm (up to ~0.013), and 1630 nm (up to ~0.016), and negative troughs around 1120 nm (down to ~-0.009), 1360 nm (down to ~-0.011), and 1700 nm (dropping below -0.025). In plot (c), the y-axis is labeled "PLS regression coefficient" with ticks at -0.00075, -0.0005, -0.00025, 0, 0.00025, 0.0005, and 0.00075; a single black curve starts near 0.00068 at 900 nm, shows local minima around 980 nm (~0.00053) and 1200 nm (~0.0002), crosses the 0 line just past 1300 nm, reaches a broad global minimum around 1450 nm (~-0.00078), and rises back across 0 near 1680 nm to reach ~0.00025 at 1700 nm.
